# Experimental data

Files in this directory support research and pipeline checks. They are not the operational catalog or production training data.

## Experience ranking

The ranking experiment uses OSM-derived places, six assumed intent profiles, and weak labels generated by rules. Its results indicate how well a model reproduces those rules; they do not establish real-user preference quality.

See the [ranking experiment notes](ranking/README.md).

## Flood-report experiment

flood_training_weak_labels.csv is an experiment about the appearance of public flood reports. Some negative examples are created by sampling locations/months without a matching report in the source. They are not verified no-flood observations and must not be used as physical road-flood labels for the planner.

Keep estimated values and provenance visible. Do not use these artifacts to claim production performance or verified real-world labels.
